Description

Build the Market Leader in Satellite Analytics with us at LiveEO You make sure LiveEO has the right Earth observation data to power our infrastructure monitoring solutions. You work closely with satellite data providers and with our internal teams to plan and secure the imagery we need, and you build the tools that make this work efficient. As the data acquisition expert in the room, you enable our sales and product teams to scope solutions for our customers. You will support our growth and shape our long-term data strategy and how we work with the space industry.

Your challenge

  • Plan and order commercial satellite imagery from optical, SAR, thermal and elevation providers.
  • Develop data acquisition strategies that balance coverage, quality, timeline and cost.
  • Design and coordinate large-scale tasking campaigns.
  • Translate customer and product requirements into feasible acquisition plans.
  • Act as technical contact for satellite data providers and platform partners.
  • Build tools and dashboards that support acquisition planning.
  • Keep documentation on providers, data products and workflows up to date.
  • Communicate with internal teams and external partners, acting as technical contact for satellite data providers and internal stakeholders.

Your profile

  • Degree in geoinformatics, remote sensing, geodesy, computer science or a related field.
  • Hands-on experience with commercial Earth observation data and satellite tasking.
  • Good understanding of EO satellite tasking and image products.
  • Strong Python skills, ideally with geospatial libraries.
  • Solid knowledge of geospatial data formats and coordinate reference systems.
  • Familiarity with STAC and API-based data access.
  • Structured, detail-oriented way of working.
  • Strong communication skills and comfort working with internal teams and external partners.
  • Familiar with agentic coding.
  • Business-fluent English and German.
  • Experience with AWS services such as Lambda and S3 is a plus.
  • Background in SAR or elevation data products is a plus.
  • Interest in infrastructure monitoring applications is a plus.
  • Good understanding of multi-sensor acquisition planning is a plus.
