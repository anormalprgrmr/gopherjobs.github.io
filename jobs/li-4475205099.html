Description

The private equity and asset management industry manages trillions in assets, funding massive innovation and high-impact companies around the world. Most of this still operates on manual PDF, email and paper-based processes and fragmented legacy systems. Vestlane replaces that with a digital-native onboarding and compliance experience: fund and asset managers as well as administrators and other stakeholders use the platform to onboard investors, assets and other counterparties, run compliant KYC/AML processes, and manage subscription documents end to end.

We are a lean tech company operating at the intersection of RegTech, LegalTech, and FinTech with the mission to transform this industry from point-in-time fundraising to perpetually compliant KYC/AML lifecycle operations.

We’re hiring a Software Engineer to join our international team and extend Vestlane with Angular/TypeScript, Spring/Kotlin, and MongoDB. You’ll work across the platform, from UI components to public APIs.

Tasks

Vestlane includes a mobile-friendly web app, a low-code workflow engine, and public APIs, with more features on the roadmap.

  • Design, develop, and operate new features with TypeScript/Angular, Spring/Kotlin, MongoDB.

  • Maintain mission-critical components of the existing platform.

  • Deliver work end to end, from collaborating on specifications to verification.
