Description

Our Mission

Our mission with MeinGPT: be the AI use case platform for the Mittelstand - the system that gets companies from their first AI experiment to AI running in their core processes.

MeinGPT already serves 50,000+ users from 500+ companies. What comes next isn't just more users. It's more depth: AI that connects to real data, real systems, and real workflows. That demands an architecture that doesn't get in the way. Platform Engineers build the foundation that makes it all possible - reliably, securely, and at any scale.

tl;dr: Munich/Unterhaching or Berlin • 40h/week • EUR 60-90k

Your Mission

AI products integrate, move, and process more data per user action than most SaaS: external sources synced continuously, context assembled in real time from multiple systems, streaming responses held coherent across distributed services, LLM requests routed with fallback and cost logic across providers. Getting this right, under real load, for real customers, is an architecture problem. Platform Engineers build and own that foundation.

The work is proactive: you read the system's signals (latency distributions, cost curves, error rates) and act before they become user problems. The goal isn't to keep pace with growth. It's to build a platform that's more capable and more reliable at 100,000 users than it is today.

What We Are Looking For

  • 3+ years TypeScript in production backend systems - your focus is clearly on the backend, but you're comfortable in the frontend too and can jump in when needed
  • You think about systems under sustained load: how they degrade, what breaks first at 2x traffic, where the cost curve goes - and you design for that, not just for today's requirements.
  • Strong system design knowledge: PostgreSQL, APIs, queues, caching. You know when to use what and why.
  • Experience with containerized deployments (Docker, Kubernetes) and the operational practices that come with them.
  • When you see a latency pattern, cost spike, or error rate trend, you track it to its root and fix it properly - not apply a patch and call it a day.
  • When data is missing for well-founded decisions, you add the instrumentation yourself.
  • You share what you know (context, tradeoffs, open questions) and make the engineers around you better at their work.

  • Work in a novel, data-intensive field with interesting scaling challenges: making terabytes of data available to AI on-demand, providing compute infrastructure for AI agents at scale, and making MeinGPT a flexible platform to build AI applications on.
