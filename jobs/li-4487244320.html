Description

What You'll Be Working On

  • Within the backend team, you're the go-to person for AI and work closely with Product.
  • You further develop our AI agents and automation solutions such as Connect AI, from the agent loop and tool calling to context management.
  • You help shape our AI architecture and make sure we can work flexibly with different LLMs.
  • When it comes to quality, we rely on evals with real customer conversations and methods like LLM-as-a-judge. You help expand this area so every new version gets measurably better.
  • Through APIs, MCP tools and website crawling, you give our agents the knowledge and tools they need.

What Makes You a Fit

  • You have at least five years of backend experience, including one year building AI features based on language models that people use every day.
  • You know Python and ideally TypeScript.
  • You’ve built AI agents before and know context windows, tool calling and prompting from hands-on work. Agent libraries like Pydantic AI might already be part of your toolkit.
  • You’ve worked with RAG and vector databases yourself. Evals and observability are familiar ground for you, whether with Langfuse, Arize Phoenix or a setup you built yourself.
  • Experience with AI gateways like Bifrost or LiteLLM is a nice extra.
  • You follow what’s happening in AI, try things out quickly and take ownership of your topics.
  • You’re fluent in German and English.
