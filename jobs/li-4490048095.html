Description

🤓 About Luminovo At Luminovo, we’re building the infrastructure that turns the electronics supply chain into something software can read and act on. Our customers use it to quote PCBAs faster, find cost savings and act on risks in their supply chain.

We serve 300+ customers, from Europe’s largest EMS to category-leading OEMs, with around 100 Luminerds across Munich, Berlin, New York and Austin. We’re backed by Chalfen Ventures, Cherry Ventures and General Catalyst. With data centers and robotics driving demand for electronics, someone needs to manage that supply chain, and they’ll need good software to do it. (Here’s why we care so much about electronics.)

🚀 Your role Team Core is our platform team. Its job is to make all our other engineering teams more productive and to improve our codebase and infrastructure as a whole. You’ll:

  • Keep our DevOps setup and CI/CD pipelines fast and reliable
  • Build tools every team needs (e.g. interface for building permissions)
  • Act as an in-house Rust expert: our backend is a Rust monolith, and Team Core helps the other teams use good Rust patterns and architecture
  • Be responsible for the Developer Experience (DX).
  • Help our engineers use agents more productively

Other Details

  • Location: remote-first, within two hours of CET. We prefer Munich > Berlin > Germany > Europe.
  • Tech stack: Rust, TypeScript + React, Postgres, Clickhouse, Redis, Kafka, Kubernetes, Helm, Flux

🖲️ Our tech stack We always try to use the best tools available for the job. Don't worry, we don't need you to be familiar with all of these:

  • Main monolith is written in Rust (using actix-web as our web framework), deployed on Kubernetes
  • React and Typescript for frontend
  • PostgreSQL for persisting our data
  • Redis for caching and queues
  • Flux for GitOps
  • Honeycomb, Prometheus and Grafana for monitoring and observability
  • garden-io for developing on Kubernetes
  • Azure and Cloudflare for cloud computing

  • Timon, one of our Founders

  • Inga, Senior Software Engineer
