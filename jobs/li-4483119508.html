Description

SteerSuite HQ runs on Azure and goes to market this fall. You own the technology behind it: architecture, operations and security. You share this with Nemanja Milenkovic, who's already on the team as a founding engineer. When a customer's IT team checks whether they can trust us with their data, you're their point of contact.

Who we are

SteerSuite makes companies AI-native. With SteerSuite HQ we're building a platform for a plug & play AI workforce: AI agents anyone can put to work on their own tasks in a few steps, without technical skills. Founded in 2026 by Sakis Tabakis and Julian Rosen, based in Bochum/Düsseldorf and Miami.

What you'll do

  • Own the architecture: a multi-tenant platform on Azure and PostgreSQL, with tenant isolation through row-level security.
  • Operations and security: alerting, incidents, rollback. You handle security questionnaires, pentests and customer reviews from the first enterprise conversation on.
  • Sign-off: you and Nemanja decide what ships to production.
  • Code reviews and quality standards across the team.

Who you are

  • You've run a multi-tenant SaaS in production, incidents included.
  • You know Azure, PostgreSQL and row-level security from hands-on work.
  • You've been through a security audit or a customer due diligence.
  • AI tools are part of how you build software every day.

Nice to have, not required: ISO 27001 or SOC 2 experience.

What you get

  • Technical ownership of a product that goes to market this fall.
  • A direct line to both founders. You manage your own time.
  • Equity: we'll discuss it before the first conversation.
  • Compensation: we'll share the range before the first interview.

The setup

Part-time or full-time (50–100%), you tell us what fits. Start immediately, remote from Germany, occasional on-site in Bochum or Düsseldorf, we cover travel costs. The role is built to last and grows with the engineering team.
