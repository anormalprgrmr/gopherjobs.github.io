Start: Immediately

Working student: 15–20 hours per week

Would you like to gain practical software engineering experience alongside your studies—or write your Bachelor’s or Master’s thesis together with an established technology company?

Our client is a German software and AI company with strong academic roots. Founded by a university professor, the company has grown to around 150 employees while retaining a research-driven culture focused on technical quality, continuous learning and solving complex problems.

Its engineering teams develop modern digital products and AI solutions used by major organisations across Germany, particularly within the financial sector.

You can join through one of two routes:

  • Work as a Working Student Software Engineer
  • Complete your Bachelor’s or Master’s thesis with the company

What you could work on

  • Contributing to real software products and customer projects
  • Exploring open-source libraries and modern frameworks
  • Supporting the full software lifecycle—from analysis and design through to development, testing and rollout
  • Working with object-oriented design and agile development practices
  • Selecting suitable technologies for your project or thesis
  • Supporting CI/CD pipelines and development automation
  • Taking ownership of a defined technical topic with guidance from experienced engineers

What you should bring

  • Current enrolment in Computer Science, Business Informatics or a comparable subject
  • Some initial experience in web or software development
  • Basic knowledge of at least one of the following:

Java, Spring, TypeScript, JavaScript, Angular, Node.js, Ruby on Rails, databases, Docker or Kubernetes

  • An analytical mindset and enthusiasm for solving complex problems
  • Fluent German—minimum C1 level—and good English
  • Residence in Germany

What’s on offer

  • 100% remote working within Germany
  • Flexible hours that fit around your studies
  • Support from highly qualified software engineers
  • Training and professional development opportunities
  • Experience working on real, technically challenging products
  • The potential to move into permanent employment after your studies
