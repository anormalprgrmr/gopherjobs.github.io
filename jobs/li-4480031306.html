Description

At Nect, we make digitalization tangible and secure. We develop AI- and biometrics-based trust services, including digital identity and signature solutions. Sounds technical? It is - but, more importantly, it’s exciting. Our technologies help public authorities and businesses prepare for the digital future. We are driven by our desire to demonstrate the simplicity and security of digital solutions while creating new opportunities for all. Are you ready to help shape the digital world of tomorrow with us?

About the role: At Nect, frontend and backend work hand in hand to guide users through complex identification processes in a simple and secure way. As a Full-Stack Engineer, you combine both worlds in one role and take ownership of features end-to-end, from user experience and technical design to implementation and operations.

As part of our Engineering team you collaborate with Product Managers, QA Managers, our Computer Vision and Machine Learning experts directly and daily. Together with all these disciplines you will build the best Ident app in the market!

You are responsible for:

  • Product development: Work with the product team to build new features and improve existing ones to make life better for users and customers.
  • Own technical solutions: Convert product requirements into scalable technical designs and align them with engineering, product, UX, and QA stakeholders.
  • Build great frontend experiences: Develop modern React/TypeScript applications, create reusable components, and ensure our products are intuitive and enjoyable.
  • Develop reliable backend services: Design and implement Go and React/TypeScript services that power our products and business processes.
  • Ensure quality from the start: Collaborate with QA and UX throughout development to deliver high-quality solutions.
  • Keep our systems secure: Own application security, dependency management, deployment pipelines, and operational excellence.
  • Operations: You build it, you run it. Monitor, maintain, and continuously improve the services you own in production.

You bring with you:

  • Excellent experience in full-stack software engineering, with hands-on exposure to both frontend and backend development.
  • Experience with modern frontend frameworks and technologies (e.g. TypeScript, React, Next.js, JavaScript).
  • Experience building APIs and backend services, ideally using Go.
  • Experience developing customer-facing products used directly by end users.
  • Clear communication skills.
  • Excellent collaboration skills.
  • You have a strong sense of software quality, maintainability, and user experience.
  • Fluent English required.

What we offer you:

  • Flexible working hours & remote working
  • Scale-up culture with security and vision: digital, innovative, ambitious
  • Flat hierarchies and quick decisions: real scope for your ideas
  • Personal development: in a growing tech company with a future
  • Modern office in Hamburg: central location between the Elbe and Alster rivers with a pleasant working atmosphere
  • Health benefits: company health insurance package
  • Team spirit: regular events and exchanges in a motivated environment
  • Catering support: subsidy for your daily meals/snacks
