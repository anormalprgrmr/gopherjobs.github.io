Senior Backend Engineer (Backend)

📍 Berlin (hybrid) | High-growth consumer marketplace scale-up

About the company

Our client is a well-funded, international scale-up running a two-sided online marketplace that connects consumers with thousands of business partners. Technology sits at the centre of the business, and the engineering team is growing to support the next stage of growth.

The role

You’ll join a cross-functional product team of engineers, product managers and designers. The team works on core marketplace products, from partner-facing tools and infrastructure to search, discovery and customer-facing features used at global scale.

What you’ll do

  • Design, build and scale distributed backend systems
  • Work closely with product, design and data colleagues to ship features and learn from experiments
  • Improve code quality, system reliability and engineering processes
  • Mentor engineers and help shape hiring
  • Work across a modern stack including Java, Spring Boot, Kafka, Kubernetes, SQL databases and modern frontend frameworks

What you’ll bring

  • 6+ years of software engineering experience, ideally with distributed systems or search
  • Strong Java skills
  • Experience designing and running large-scale, reliable, observable systems
  • A practical approach to using AI tools, and the ability to help your team adopt them responsibly
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills
  • A collaborative, mentoring mindset and a data-driven, customer-focused approach
  • Fluent English

Nice to have

  • A/B testing and product analytics experience
  • Some frontend experience (e.g. TypeScript or a component-based framework)
  • E-commerce, marketplace or scale-up background

What’s on offer

  • Competitive salary and equity
  • Learning and development budget
  • Hybrid working with flexible remote options
  • Team and company events
  • Wellbeing, fitness and travel benefits
