Description

We are CARIAD, the automotive software company of the Volkswagen Group. Our teams build automotive software platforms and digital customer functions for iconic brands like Audi, Volkswagen, and Porsche – supporting the Volkswagen Group in becoming the leading automotive technology company. With CARIDIANS in Germany, the USA, China, Estonia, and India, we are transforming automotive mobility for everyone.

Join us and be part of this exciting journey!

YOUR TEAM To support our PRISMA team, we are currently looking for an intern. As part of our interdisciplinary team, you will gain first-hand experience in user research, product validation, and user-centered product development in the automotive context.

We combine research, simulation, and prototyping to understand user needs, validate requirements and concepts, and support product teams in making evidence-based decisions. You will work closely with researchers, designers, developers, and product teams and actively contribute to real-world validation activities.

What You Will Do

  • Planning and execution of user research (usability tests, online surveys, simulator studies …)
  • Technical setup and configuration of seating bucks and driving simulations
  • Analysis and visualization of research findings in PowerPoint and Power BI
  • Moderation of workshops to integrate the research findings into our products
  • Organization and execution of user testing events with external partners
  • Integration and technical support of prototypes for validations and demonstrations
  • Documentation of research findings, processes, and technical validation setups

Who You Are

  • Enrolled student in the area of Human Factors, Psychology, Computer Science, or similar
  • Strong interest in UX, user research, digital products, voice interfaces, and prototyping
  • Independent, structured, and hands-on way of working
  • Strong communication and interviewing skills and first experience with data analysis
  • Interest in technical prototyping, programming, or physical computing
  • Very good German and English skills; additional languages are welcome

NICE TO KNOW

  • Duration: 6 months
  • 35 hours/week
  • Salary: 13,90 €/hour
