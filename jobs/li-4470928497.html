Description

About Us Base360.ai is the AI operating system for short-term rental businesses, helping property managers run their operations on autopilot.

Born out of The Flex, a short-term rental operator that scaled to an eight-figure business using its own software, Base360.ai is now launching as a standalone platform for short-term rental operators worldwide.

We bring reservations, guest communication, pricing, payments, cleaning, maintenance, and reporting into one platform—helping operators automate manual work, reduce costs, increase revenue, and scale their portfolios.

We're looking for a highly ambitious Senior Software Engineer who wants to build systems that solve real problems at scale. You'll build, ship, automate, and deliver products that directly impact property managers, their teams, and their guests.

If you're excited by APIs, automation, AI agents, cloud infrastructure, and building products from the ground up, we want to meet you.

What You'll Build

As a Senior Software Engineer, you'll help architect and scale Base360.ai—the platform powering every aspect of a short-term rental business.

You'll build customer-facing products, automation engines, AI-powered workflows, integrations, and operational systems that help property managers grow without increasing their workload at the same rate.

Your work will span the entire short-term rental lifecycle: from listing distribution, pricing, and bookings to guest stays, property operations, and financial reporting.

Your Mission

Build the Core Platform

Design and develop scalable applications, services, and APIs that power reservations, availability, pricing, payments, guest experiences, and property operations.

Create Powerful Automations

Build event-driven workflows that eliminate repetitive work—from scheduling cleaning after a booking to sending check-in instructions and generating owner statements.

Develop AI-Powered Systems

Use LLMs and AI agents to automate guest communication, triage maintenance requests, support pricing decisions, and help operators manage their portfolios more efficiently.

Design API-First Architecture

Create robust APIs that connect internal services, booking channels, third-party providers, and customer systems.

Integrate the Ecosystem

Build and maintain integrations with platforms including Airbnb, Hostaway, Stripe, Twilio, OpenAI, Google, and other strategic partners across the short-term rental ecosystem.

Scale Infrastructure

Deploy and manage cloud-native infrastructure designed for reliability, performance, and global growth across multiple property management businesses.
