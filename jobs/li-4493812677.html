The Role Job Title: AI Full Stack Engineer (m/f/d)

Location: Berlin, Germany

Why this role matters An AI model is only as valuable as the user experience and reliable systems delivering its output. This role bridges advanced machine learning and real-world utility by transforming complex business objectives into production-ready platforms. Without your full-stack engineering expertise, state-of-the-art AI solutions remain trapped in prototypes rather than solving critical industry problems.

What You'll Do You will own the end-to-end full-stack architecture, deployment, and user experience for production-ready AI applications.

  • Design and develop production-grade, AI-augmented information systems solving complex industry challenges.
  • Build data platforms for integration, curation, exploration, and monitoring at scale.
  • Develop and maintain scalable cloud applications across AWS and GCP environments.
  • Construct and optimize robust RESTful and GraphQL APIs connecting frontend interfaces with backend services.
  • Collaborate with ML, data, and infrastructure engineers to co-create architecture and deliver product milestones.
  • Identify product improvements proactively, challenge existing approaches, and propose high-value technical solutions.

What success looks like Month 1: Ramp up rapidly into a new client engagement, integrating with your dedicated engineering squad in the on-site project room.

Month 2-3: Co-create core application architecture and deliver critical full-stack product milestones alongside ML and data engineers.

By end of Q1: Establish fully operational, production-ready full-stack services integrated with scalable cloud infrastructure and AI models.

Who This Is a Fit For

  • Proven full-stack engineer proficient in Python backends and TypeScript, React, HTML5, and CSS3 frontends.
  • Experienced architect of RESTful and GraphQL APIs for complex data workflows.
  • Hands-on operator deploying and maintaining scalable infrastructure on AWS or GCP.
  • Solid database practitioner skilled across relational (PostgreSQL/MySQL) and NoSQL data stores.
  • Fast learner who ramps up quickly in new technical and domain environments.
  • Collaborative communicator energized by 5 days/week in-person problem-solving in Berlin.

What it's like to work here The company fosters an entrepreneurial startup culture built on high ownership, flat hierarchies, and 360-degree open feedback. For this dedicated project, the engineering squad works closely together in a shared project room at our Berlin office, emphasizing immediate feedback loops, intense co-located collaboration, rapid problem-solving, and regular AI community events.

The offer

  • Intense, co-located collaboration in a dedicated project room at the AI Campus Berlin.
  • Startup culture built on high ownership, flat hierarchies, and 360-degree open feedback.
  • Immediate feedback loops working side-by-side with ML, data, and infrastructure specialists.
  • Access to regular on-site AI community events and cutting-edge industry initiatives.

This probably isn't for you if

  • You want remote or hybrid work; this project strictly requires 5 days/week on-site presence in Berlin.
  • You prefer long-term permanent contracts right away rather than a 7-month fixed-term engagement (Jan–July 2027) with extension potential.
  • You want isolated task-based tickets rather than cross-functional co-creation and challenging the status quo.
