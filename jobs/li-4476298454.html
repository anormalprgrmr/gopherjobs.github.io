Join us in making a difference

Our Hybrid Platform team builds and runs the foundation trivago's hotel search depends on: our Google Cloud environment, our own data centres, and the network that connects them to each other and our campus. As Site Reliability Engineering Lead, you'll lead a team of eight SREs and network engineers, reporting to our Head of SRE Platform, set its direction, and keep that foundation secure, reliable and ready for what comes next. This isn't a purely managerial role: you'll stay close to the technology, shape designs, tackle hard problems and help when things get tricky. You'll build a system the team can rely on, with clear reliability targets, automation over toil and sustainable on-call, and a culture where honest feedback and blameless learning are the norm. trivago is a place where ambition and support go hand in hand. Our leads invest time in AI workflows every day and help their teams grow with new tools.

Ready to shape our hybrid future? Apply now!

How you'll make an impact:

  • Own the strategy and roadmap for our hybrid infrastructure across GCP, data centre and campus, deciding which workloads run where and explaining the trade-offs clearly to leadership and non-technical stakeholders.
  • Stay hands-on where it counts: review and shape architecture, get involved in critical troubleshooting, and contribute to the automation and infrastructure as code your team relies on.
  • Keep the platform secure, available, performant and cost-efficient, with clear reliability targets the team and its users can measure against.
  • Make the platform easy for product teams to use, with self-service where it makes sense, clear interfaces and documentation people actually read.
  • Build a healthy on-call and incident culture: sustainable rotations, blameless postmortems, and root causes that get fixed.
  • Manage hardware lifecycle, vendors, contracts and the team's budget across data centre, network and cloud.
  • Build a winning team by leading hiring, coaching and career development, and by giving regular, honest feedback so the team has the right capabilities to drive transformation.
  • Create clarity on team priorities, translate strategic objectives into actionable plans and team OKRs, and hold the team accountable for steady execution, staying agile as priorities evolve.

What you'll need to thrive:

  • Hands-on experience across cloud (ideally GCP) and on-premise data centre and network infrastructure, including a solid understanding of TCP/IP, routing protocols such as BGP and OSPF, and modern data centre architectures such as IP fabric.
  • Proven experience leading, coaching or mentoring engineering teams in infrastructure, network or platform engineering, with a track record of helping others grow while delivering results.
  • The ability to adapt your leadership style situationally, knowing when to direct, coach, support or delegate based on each person's needs, and the confidence to communicate with both engineers and executives.
  • A genuine learning mindset: you approach new technologies and challenges with curiosity, seek out feedback, and keep developing yourself and your team.
  • A performance mindset: you set ambitious reliability goals, base decisions on data about capacity, cost and reliability, and have the resilience to see them through, including as part of the escalation path for critical incidents.
  • A hands-on, daily commitment to using AI tools in real workflows, building on experience with infrastructure as code (Terraform, Ansible or similar), Kubernetes and CI/CD, and a drive to enable your team to do the same, treating AI fluency as a core leadership expectation, not an add-on.

Stand out with:

  • Experience with Juniper routers, switches and firewalls.
  • Google Cloud networking and hybrid connectivity, such as Interconnect and VPN.
  • Experience with data centre hardware, colocation and hardware vendor management.
  • Cloud cost management or FinOps experience.
  • Experience leading migrations between on-prem and cloud, in either direction.
  • Open source involvement.
