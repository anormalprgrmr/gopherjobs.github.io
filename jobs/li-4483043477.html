Description

Short and crisp Are you passionate about building modern software and creating great digital experiences for wealth management clients? Do you enjoy taking ownership, turning business requirements into robust solutions, and working with a collaborative team in a fast-moving environment? Welcome to aixigo!

Your job – Your passion

  • You take ownership of our clients’ applications on the BLOXX wealth management platform — from architecture to delivery, for both standard platform solutions and individual customisations
  • Designing and implementing business services using modern REST and event-driven approaches, including OpenAPI/Swagger, JAX-RS and Apache Kafka is one of your daily tasks
  • You drive frontend development with Vue.js, creating clean, component-based and high-performing user interfaces
  • You work closely with an interdisciplinary team of consultants, software developers and architects
  • In code reviews, mentoring and technical decision-making you contribute your experience and help the whole team grow
  • You develop in short, agile iterations using GitLab CI, automated testing and a quality-first mindset
  • You work in a modern cloud and operational environment with Kubernetes, Docker, Rancher & Co.
  • You help shape our platform architecture and further develop our partner integration capabilities

Your profile – Your strengths

  • With several years of professional experience as a full stack developer, you are ready to contribute from day one
  • You have strong Java skills, ideally including asynchronous and reactive programming patterns such as Futures, RxJava or similar
  • Working with REST, OpenAPI, JAX-RS, JPA/Hibernate, Git and Maven is one of your strengths
  • You have hands-on, production-level experience with Vue.js and a solid understanding of JavaScript, HTML and CSS
  • You have worked with Apache Kafka or other event-driven messaging systems
  • Software quality is important to you and you are experienced with JUnit/TestNG, integration testing and test-driven development
  • You can assess architectural trade-offs, make sound technical decisions and communicate them clearly
  • You enjoy working with clients, understand their business context and can prioritise accordingly
  • Communicating in English, both spoken and written is routine for you. German skills are a plus

Our offer to you

  • Great creative freedom and a variety of opportunities for further development in a very trusting and appreciative environment
  • The choice between different work models (remote, office or hybrid) with flexible working hours, a working time account, overtime regulations and modern and ergonomically equipped workstations
  • State-of-the-art collaboration tools for efficient hybrid working
  • A family-friendly and relaxed atmosphere where ideas are created together and quickly implemented thanks to the flat hierarchy
  • Unlimited employment contracts, company pension plan, cafeteria, health campaigns, dogs at the workplace, sufficient and free parking spaces, bicycle leasing, employee PC program, mobile tire service, various events, parcel service, drinks, fresh fruit and vegetables, cookies and much more!
