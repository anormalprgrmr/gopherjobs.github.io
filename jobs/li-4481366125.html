Description

why this role matters

As Lead Software Engineer at coera Robotics, you will work directly alongside the CTO to shape and build our core safety intelligence platform, enabling humans and robots to work safely together. You will take end-to-end technical ownership, from software architecture and hands-on C++ development to delivering our first certified release. Along the way, you will establish the engineering foundations, tools, and practices that will support our growing team and turn complex safety requirements into reliable, production-ready software.

what you will own

  • Lead the architecture, design, and hands-on development of coera's core safety intelligence platform, from runtime through integration and tooling.
  • Write and review production-quality C++ code, solve complex technical challenges, and establish high engineering standards.
  • Drive software delivery towards our first certifiable release and customer deployment, working against functional safety requirements (SIL 2 / PL d). Build and continuously
  • improve the engineering toolchain, including testing, CI, simulation, and validation practices.
  • Collaborate closely with functional safety specialists, embedded systems engineers, and external partners to translate certification requirements into practical engineering solutions.
  • Own technical priorities, make informed trade-offs, and keep development aligned with critical product and certification milestones.
  • Help shape the growing engineering team through technical guidance, knowledge sharing, and participation in hiring.

what we look forMust have

  • Strong professional experience developing and shipping production software in modern C++.
  • Deep understanding of embedded systems, real-time constraints, and software architecture.
  • Experience working with sensor-based, perception, robotics, or similarly complex systems.
  • Proven ability to take end-to-end technical ownership, from architecture through implementation and delivery.
  • Hands-on technical leadership, including code reviews, technical decision-making, and guiding other engineers. Ability to operate effectively in a fast-moving environment, challenge assumptions constructively, and deliver against demanding timelines.

About us

coera is building the universal safety intelligence layer for human-robot coexistence, redefining what safety means in the age of physical AI. Robotics is advancing faster than the safety systems meant to support it. coera closes that gap with multimodal sensing and deterministic guardrails: retrofittable, embeddable, controller-agnostic. Safety is not a checkpoint or a constraint. It is the capability that lets intelligent machines operate freely wherever people do.
