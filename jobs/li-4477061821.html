Description

We are CARIAD, the automotive software company of the Volkswagen Group. Our teams build automotive software platforms and digital customer functions for iconic brands like Audi, Volkswagen, and Porsche – supporting the Volkswagen Group in becoming the leading automotive technology company. With CARIDIANS in Germany, the USA, China, Estonia, and India, we are transforming automotive mobility for everyone.

Join us and be part of this exciting journey!

YOUR TEAM To support our Parking team, we are currently looking for a working student. In your role you get first-hand experience in Parking Function Development, Requirements Engineering and AI-driven Engineering Solutions. You will be an integral part of the team that works on next-generation Parking Functions for VW Group.

What You Will Do

  • Contribute to innovative Parking Functions for future vehicles
  • Coordinate and support cross-functional technical activities
  • Create and maintain requirements and test artifacts in Codebeamer
  • Develop scripts for process automation and engineering efficiency
  • Build smart dashboards for project and quality transparency
  • Explore and implement AI, GenAI and Agentic AI solutions
  • Support project reporting and stakeholder communication

Who You Are

  • Enrolled student in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Software Engineering or similar
  • Passion for automotive software and emerging AI technologies
  • Basic knowledge of Python, scripting or software development
  • Interest in AI Agents, automation and data-driven engineering
  • Fluent German language skills
  • Strong analytical and structured mindset

NICE TO KNOW

  • Presence in Ingolstadt and willingness to work at least 3 days/week on-site
  • Remote work options within Germany
  • Duration: 6 months (with the option to extend up to two years)
  • 20 h/week, up to 35 h/week during the semester break
  • Salary: 17,80€/hour
