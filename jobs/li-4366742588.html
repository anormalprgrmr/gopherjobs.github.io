Your Mission

  • Build production-grade applications with React, Next.js, TypeScript and Node.js – from UI to APIs and services.
  • Design AI-native experiences: conversational UIs, agent-driven workflows and natural-language interactions.
  • Use AI as an engineering multiplier. Explore, prototype, implement, debug and test faster – while staying firmly in control of architecture, quality and the final result.
  • Understand the customer, the business and the problem before deciding what to build.
  • Challenge assumptions, simplify complexity and turn ambiguity into products people actually use.
  • Shape scalable architectures, APIs and data structures across the stack.
  • Collaborate with Product, UX/UI and Engineering to make better decisions together.
  • Raise the bar for testing, performance, maintainability and engineering quality.

You’re not here to turn tickets into components. You’re here to decide what should be built – and then build it.

Your Skills

  • Think and work AI-native. You already use AI as part of your engineering workflow and have developed your own approach to getting from idea to production-ready code.
  • Bring 5+ years of hands-on full-stack JavaScript experience and a strong track record of shipping real applications.
  • Master React, Next.js, TypeScript and Node.js and understand modern web architectures.
  • Make confident technical decisions around architecture, performance, scalability and maintainability.
  • Build and test production-grade software. Hands-on experience with Jest, Vitest or Node.js testing is essential.
  • Work confidently with APIs and data: REST, database design and SQL; GraphQL is a plus.
  • Think beyond the code. You’re curious about customers, products and the business and use that context to make better technical decisions.
  • Take ownership of outcomes , not just implementation.
  • Stay curious about what’s next. Experience with AI-first interfaces, agent workflows or natural-language products is a plus.

Bonus: AWS, Jenkins, Grafana, Graylog, Confluence, Jira, shell scripting.
