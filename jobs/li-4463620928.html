Description

At go-e, we have been pursuing the development of our vision with passion and strength from the very beginning. We are moving in an area that is characterized by constant technological evolution, supporting transition toward more sustainable mobility and cleaner energy sources.

We are looking for an innovative and driven Software Engineer (C++, Linux) who shares our vision in quality and commits to building outstanding products. Our products combine in-house developed hardware with bare metal firmware, a rich embedded Linux environment and multiple user-facing frontend applications to provide the best user experience to our customers.

With your contribution, you will impact how people will charge their cars in the future. Come and join the team that is building the future of innovative charger solutions for e-mobility!
