Description

We're looking for an experienced software engineer to join secunet's Internal Developer Platform.

We value fast and incremental improvements. Your software development efforts will focus on designing and building infrastructure, optimizing systems, and eliminating work through automation.

The Platform is secunet's innovation program, designed to provide the technical means to develop entirely new product classes. It consists of a team of highly motivated software engineers focused on cutting-edge, security-focused shared components, and a DevSecOps platform that supports the entire lifecycle of our products.

  • NixOS

  • GitLab

Your Responsibilities

  • Maintain, extend, and continuously improve our infrastructure
  • Ensure solid observability across all our services (visualization, alerting, log aggregation, tracing)
  • Coordinate with internal teams, and users of the Platform
  • Document your work, both for users of the infrastructure as well as for fellow developers
  • Ensure the security of our services

Your Profile

  • University degree in computer science or a related field. Alternatively, you have roughly equivalent experience to a university degree
  • Excellent problem-solving and communication skills
  • Extensive experience with architecting and operating deployments
  • Experience with Nix and NixOS
  • Experience with Infrastructure as Code (e.g. Terraform)
  • Familiarity with Kubernetes
  • Programming experience in Rust, Python, Go or similar languages
  • Experience building Git Forge CI Pipelines
  • Proficient in English (German is an optional bonus)
  • Open source contributions are a nice bonus
