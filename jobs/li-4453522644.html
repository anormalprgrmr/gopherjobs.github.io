About SSC Space Welcome to SSC Space - the perfect workplace for those passionate about technology in general and space in particular.

With space organizations, research institutes, institutional and commercial actors as customers, we offer specialist expertise in satellite communications, space consulting services, rocket and balloon systems, flight test services, satellite propulsion systems, and much more.

SSC also has one of the world’s largest global networks of ground stations for satellite communications. We own and operate the Esrange Space Center in Kiruna, northern Sweden, with the aim of launching satellites in the near future.

Are you fueled by a passion for people and the ambition to create an awesome workplace? Are you ready to embark on an exciting journey with us to help earth benefit from space?

  • extensive experience with Java (21), Spring/Spring Boot, Apache Kafka, and GraphQL services.
  • experienced in building SPAs with Angular (or similar), ideally with Apollo Angular for GraphQL
  • Understanding of enterprise integration patterns, including service buses, API-based integration, messaging systems, and event-driven architectures.
  • Hands-on experience with Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD tools (Git, GitLab, Nexus).
  • Good understanding of microservices, cloud-native design, and enterprise integrations.
  • Experience with SecDevOps principles or strong security-focused development practices.
  • Familiarity with Linux, scripting (Bash/Python), and DevOps practices.
  • Experience with PostgreSQL and ideally Redis or TimescaleDB.
  • Basic experience with Prometheus (metrics) and Grafana (dashboards).
  • End-to-End Test Automation using a testing framework tool (e.g. Playwright) to ensure validation of critical user journeys and regression coverage.
  • Space domain knowledge on existing Ground Segment solutions used for Spacecraft Monitoring and Control.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a collaborative mindset.
  • Ability to approach technical challenges methodically and seek guidance when needed.

Responsibilities Front-end Development

  • Contribute to the development and maintenance of Angular-based user interfaces using TypeScript, HTML5, and CSS3.
  • Integrate frontend applications with backend services and GraphQL APIs using Apollo Angular.
  • Implement user interface designs and workflows based on product and UX requirements.
  • Maintain frontend code quality through testing, code reviews, and adherence to established standards.
  • Support troubleshooting and enhancement of existing frontend applications.

Back-End Development and Integration

  • Implement microservices in Java 21 using Spring Boot, and Apache Kafka.
  • Design, implement REST and GraphQL APIs to handle complex data querying needs.
  • Develop and optimize data models, access patterns, and database queries to ensure application performance, scalability, and maintainability.
  • Containerize and deploy microservices in Kubernetes with tools like Helm for packaging and version control.
  • Maintain CI/CD pipelines (Git, GitLab, Nexus), deploying to Kubernetes in private, hybrid, or public cloud environments.
  • Develop robust data flows with Postgres, Redis, and Timescale DB, optimizing for performance and security.
  • Integrate single sign-on (SSO) and user management using Keycloak.
  • Monitor and troubleshoot production systems using Prometheus, Grafana, logging, and observability tooling to ensure service health and reliability.
  • Troubleshoot and optimize for performance, reliability, and security.
  • Participate in sprint planning, daily stand-ups, and retrospectives, contributing ideas and feedback.
  • Work closely with cross-functional teams to gather requirements, create technical documentation, and ensure project milestones are met.
  • Collaborate using Atlassian, Confluence, JIRA, GitHub/Gitlab, and Microsoft Teams for issue tracking, code reviews, and knowledge sharing.
