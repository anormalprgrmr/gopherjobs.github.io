Description

This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Software Engineer - Crypto based in Germany. This role offers the opportunity to build and evolve the core technology powering large-scale crypto trading and brokerage services.

You will architect and develop exchange, market-making, wallet, funding, and tokenization systems serving millions of users.

The position combines backend engineering, distributed systems, financial technology, and crypto infrastructure.

You will work on high-throughput, low-latency systems where reliability, scalability, and software quality are critical.

You will have significant ownership over technical solutions while collaborating closely with cross-functional teams.

The environment is globally distributed, collaborative, and engineering-driven, with strong emphasis on autonomy and continuous improvement.

This is a strong opportunity for an experienced engineer who enjoys solving complex technical challenges in financial markets and crypto.

Accountabilities

  • Design, build, and maintain exchange and market-making systems supporting crypto trading at scale.
  • Develop reliable services for crypto wallets, funding, tokenization, and related financial infrastructure.
  • Architect and implement backend services and web applications using modern engineering practices.
  • Build systems capable of handling high-throughput, low-latency workloads while maintaining reliability and fault tolerance.
  • Maintain strong software quality, testing standards, code coverage, and clean, maintainable code.
  • Participate actively in code reviews and contribute to technical decision-making.
  • Investigate and resolve production incidents, technical issues, and reported bugs.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define technical goals and design solutions that meet business requirements.
  • Take part in on-call responsibilities when required to support time-sensitive engineering initiatives.

Requirements

  • 4+ years of professional experience in backend software development.
  • Proven experience building trading systems, exchanges, matching engines, market-making platforms, or other high-throughput, low-latency financial systems.
  • Hands-on experience developing crypto-related products or infrastructure.
  • Strong knowledge of financial markets and regulated, fault-tolerant systems.
  • Professional experience with at least one modern programming language such as Go, C, or C++; strong Go experience is particularly relevant.
  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, a related technical discipline, or equivalent practical experience.
  • Experience working with messaging queues and message brokers.
  • Solid SQL and relational database knowledge.
  • Hands-on experience with Bash, Linux, and Docker.
  • Strong focus on software quality, reliability, testing, and delivering robust solutions.
  • Comfortable challenging existing approaches, sharing technical opinions, and taking ownership of engineering decisions.
  • Experience with Kubernetes, CI/CD, DevOps practices, major cloud platforms such as AWS, GCP, or Azure, microservice architectures, Agile/Scrum, or clean-code principles is a plus.
  • Kubernetes certifications such as CKA or CKAD are also advantageous.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary and stock options.
  • Health benefits.
  • One-time USD $500 home-office setup allowance for new hires.
  • USD $150 monthly stipend provided through a Brex Card.
  • Remote-friendly working environment with a globally distributed team.
  • Opportunity to work on large-scale crypto, trading, and financial infrastructure.
  • High level of autonomy and ownership over technical solutions.
  • Collaboration with experienced engineers, traders, and financial technology professionals.
  • Exposure to complex, high-throughput systems serving users and financial institutions globally.
