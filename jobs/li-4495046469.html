Senior ML Evaluation Engineer, Berlin

This engineer owns the tests that decide which version of an AI-delivered mental health treatment goes into its first clinical pilot study. Building tests that predict real patient outcomes is still an open problem, and even a small improvement makes a difference.

Role: Senior ML Evaluation Engineer (Python, ML and LLM evaluation)

Location: Berlin, Germany (hybrid, office 3 to 4 days a week; relocation to Berlin welcome)

Salary: €70k+

Type: Permanent, full-time

Start: February 2027

Recruiter: SODA

About the company

The company is a Berlin medtech developing software that delivers psychological treatment to adults through conversation with AI. The software is a medical device in development, and a clinical pilot study will test it with real patients for the first time. The team is a small group of psychologists, engineers and scientists who work with researchers and clinicians in precision psychiatry, data science and machine learning. English is the working language because the company builds for the US market.

The role

The Senior ML Evaluation Engineer owns the evaluation, which is the set of tests that scores each version of the software before any patient uses it. Clinical outcomes take months to show and a clinical study can only test a few versions, so almost every product decision depends on the evaluation. In this role you'll:

  • design and build the evaluation in Python, including what it measures and how it runs
  • decide with the clinicians when a result is strong enough to act on
  • plan the work in steps that people outside the team can check
  • test the evaluation against the best evidence on clinical outcomes and fix the parts that predict poorly

You'll work directly with the Head of AI and lead the evaluation effort across engineering, clinical work and AI research. The role has no direct reports. A Senior Data Scientist is being hired alongside you as the main statistician.

What we're looking for

You're an engineer first. You build, ship and deploy production software hands-on, in Python or in another mainstream language you'd move from quickly, and writing code stays a large part of the job.

You've built an evaluation or test system for an ML or language-model system, such as a benchmark, tests for model outputs, a human rating process or an automated rater checked against human raters. You've checked whether a metric tracked what it was meant to, and you can tell whether a difference between two results is real or noise.

You've worked closely with experts outside engineering, ideally clinicians or psychologists, and turned their judgment into something a system measures, including challenging an expert assumption with evidence. You don't need to be a clinician, but you want to understand mental health conditions and how psychotherapy works.

You've owned a deliverable that several disciplines contributed to and led it without formal authority.

Nice to have, most important first: psychotherapy or psychology research combined with ML, clinical outcome measures, LLM-as-a-judge evaluation, safety evaluation of conversational AI, web servers and APIs, and regulated medical software (IEC 62304, ISO 13485).

Logistics

Berlin office 3 to 4 days a week, full-time. You'll need to be eligible to work in Germany by the start date, and candidates moving to Berlin are welcome. The interview process has three or four rounds and ends with an assessment day in Berlin built around a real problem from the team's work.
