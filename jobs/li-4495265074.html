The role Industrial manufacturing still runs on rigid, decades-old ERP systems and manual spreadsheets. This team is building the autonomous intelligence layer to replace them, pairing agentic reasoning with mathematical optimization and simulation to run factory operations for major global robotics and machinery builders. Backed by leading Silicon Valley venture funds and tier-one angel operators, they are tackling complex, high-stakes physical automation from Munich.

You will build the core routing, orchestration, and guardrails for systems that plan factory floors a week in advance. This is not another generic LLM wrapper or BI dashboard: what you push to production directly dictates physical plant output, requiring extreme correctness, low-latency execution, and full auditability for operators who bet their production lines on the output.

The work

  • Architect routing, orchestration, and guardrails for multi-agent systems coordinating with optimization algorithms on large-scale industrial data.
  • Own full-stack features end to end, from data pipelines and asynchronous job workflows in Python to clean, explainable frontend interfaces in TypeScript and React.
  • Implement evaluation frameworks, observability, and audit trails so factory operators can trace, trust, and verify every automated decision.
  • Work directly with plant planners to extract real operational constraints and translate complex manufacturing bottlenecks into reliable software.

What You Bring

  • Demonstrated track record of shipping production code end to end across scalable backend architecture, data pipelines, and responsive web applications.
  • Strong production fluency with modern Python (such as FastAPI, Pydantic, or Temporal) and modern TypeScript/React.
  • Experience designing or running LLM and agentic workflows where correctness, guardrails, and deterministic evaluation are mandatory.
  • Product-minded instinct to cut unused features, prioritize user workflows over pet ideas, and ship with relentless urgency.
  • Based in or willing to relocate to Munich to work on-site, with German language fluency being a strong plus.
