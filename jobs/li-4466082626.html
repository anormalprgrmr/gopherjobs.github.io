🔷 The role: Backend Engineer

Small team, high bar. You'll build the backend behind analysis work that currently takes a corporate finance team days — data pipelines, APIs and the agentic systems on top of them. You'll take features from idea to production with the motivation and ownership of a founder. We're looking for someone with at least 2 years of professional experience. Your responsibilities will include:

  • Design and ship end-to-end features used by thousands of financial professionals
  • Build backend services in Python and design clean, efficient APIs and data flows
  • Own your services in production — deployment, monitoring, and knowing first when something breaks
  • Build and maintain integrations with third-party platforms and internal services
  • Work across Elasticsearch, Qdrant and PostgreSQL , picking the right store for each problem
  • Develop agentic workflows and AI-powered pipelines
  • Contribute to critical software design and architecture decisions around scalability and reliability
  • Decide when an LLM is the right tool and when deterministic code is the better answer
  • Collaborate directly with clients and internal teams to understand needs and turn them into features

🔷 Qualifications - to be successful in this role, you will need:

Must-haves

  • We expect the motivation and ownership of a founder. You own problems end-to-end and care more about the user outcome than your ticket description
  • 2+ years of professional experience with proven track-record designing & developing scalable and reliable software
  • Proficiency in Python and strong API design skills (REST, and ideally familiarity with protocol-level tooling)
  • You ship and operate your own services — Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD , and the observability to debug production
  • You've built with LLMs in production — and know the difference between a demo and something users can actually rely on
  • You use AI/LLM tools to move faster, but you have the technical depth to audit their output and maintain high code quality. No "AI slop"
  • Familiarity with agentic orchestration frameworks (LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, or similar)
  • Experience handling complex, data-heavy backends . You know how to keep queries and pipelines fast when the data volume grows
  • Hands-on experience with PostgreSQL and with search or vector stores (Elasticsearch, Qdrant or similar)
  • Strong communication and structured problem-solving skills
  • Degree in CS, Math, Physics, or equivalent practical experience

Nice-to-haves

  • Experience in fintech or other data-intensive , regulated domains
  • Comfortable with TypeScript and a solid understanding of frontend concepts (React/Next.js)
  • Founding-experience - entrepreneurial drive , resourcefulness, and comfort with ambiguity
  • Experience with big data technologies (e.g. Apache Spark) & data orchestration tools (e.g. Dagster)
