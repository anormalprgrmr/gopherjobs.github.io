Description

About Wolt At Wolt, we create technology that brings joy, simplicity and earnings to the neighborhoods of the world. In 2014 we started with delivery of restaurant food. Now we’re building the delivery of (almost) everything and you’ll find us in over 500 cities in 30 countries around the world. In 2022 we joined forces with DoorDash and together we keep on dreaming big and expanding across the globe.

Working at Wolt isn’t always easy, but it’s definitely exciting. Here you’ll learn more, build more, and ship more than in most other companies. You’ll be challenged a lot, but also have a lot of fun on the way. So, if you’re a self-starter with drive and entrepreneurial spirit, this could be the ride of your life.

About The Team Merchant EMEA Engineering builds the technology that helps merchants join, grow, and operate successfully across our markets.

Our organization brings together the strengths of Wolt and Deliveroo and supports merchants across 37 countries. We work across the merchant journey, including onboarding, business growth and support, analytics and recommendations, menu and order management, integrations, and merchant applications.

Our teams sit close to the business. Rather than primarily building foundational platforms, we build products and features on top of our existing platforms to solve real merchant and market needs, including local business requirements, growth opportunities, and operational challenges.

📍This open role is based in our tech hubs in Helsinki or Berlin .

Please note: This role requires being based in Berlin/Helsinki on a hybrid basis. We are currently unable to offer fully remote work, visa sponsorship, or relocation support for this opportunity.

What You'll Do As a Fullstack Engineer, you'll contribute to products and services used across the merchant experience. You'll:

  • Build and maintain production software across backend services and user-facing applications.
  • Work on areas such as merchant onboarding, analytics, recommendations, menu and order management, integrations, or merchant-facing tools.
  • Develop reliable APIs, data models, services, and user-facing features.
  • Collaborate closely with product managers, designers, operations, and other engineers to turn business needs into practical technical solutions.
  • Own features from implementation through production and ongoing maintenance.
  • Contribute to technical design discussions and improve the quality, scalability, observability, and maintainability of our systems.
  • Use AI-assisted development tools effectively as part of your engineering workflow, while critically evaluating their output and taking ownership of the final solution.
  • Be willing to work outside your primary area of expertise when needed to solve the problem end-to-end.

How We Work

  • Ownership: Engineers take responsibility for the features and services they work on, from development to production.
  • Collaboration: We work closely across engineering, product, design, data, and operations.
  • Pragmatism: We value simple and maintainable solutions that create business impact without unnecessary complexity.
  • Flexibility: We care more about solving the problem than strict frontend or backend boundaries.

Our Tech Stack Our backend services primarily use Go and Kotlin , while our frontend applications use technologies such as TypeScript and React .

We also work with technologies including Kafka, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis, AWS, Kubernetes, Docker, gRPC, and modern cloud infrastructure.

Our Humble Expectations We're looking for engineers who have:

  • An AI-first mindset, with a deep experience in leveraging modern AI tools and solid engineering principles to solve problems end-to-end, independently of any specific technology stack.
  • Proven experience building production software in backend, frontend, or fullstack environments end-to-end and at scale.
  • Strong expertise in at least one area of the stack, with a willingness to contribute outside that area when needed.
  • Experience with a modern backend language such as Go, Kotlin, Java, or similar, and/or modern frontend technologies such as TypeScript and React.
  • Good understanding of APIs, databases, testing, and software design fundamentals.
  • The ability to work independently on well-scoped problems and collaborate effectively with others.
  • Good communication skills and a pragmatic, collaborative mindset.

You do not need to be equally strong in frontend and backend. We welcome different balances of experience. You might be primarily a backend engineer who is comfortable making frontend changes when needed, or a frontend-focused engineer who is happy to work with APIs and backend services.

What matters most is that you are comfortable working across boundaries to deliver the right solution.

Nice to Have Experience in one or more of the following areas would be a plus:

  • Merchant-facing or internal operational tools.
  • Analytics, recommendations, or decisioning systems.
  • Grocery, retail, restaurants, logistics, or marketplace technology.
  • Integrations with external business systems.
  • Kafka or other streaming technologies.
  • Cloud infrastructure such as AWS, Kubernetes, or Docker.

Why Join Us? You'll work on products that directly affect how merchants interact with Wolt Deliveroo and DoorDash across Europe.
