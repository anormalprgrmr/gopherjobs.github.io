Description

At justDice, we create mobile gaming experiences that reward players for their time and engagement. We move quickly, take ownership, and value people who bring ideas, curiosity, and a willingness to challenge how we do things. You’ll work with experienced teammates, solve meaningful problems, and have the freedom to make an impact from day one.

JustDice is a leading developer of mobile gaming loyalty programs. We create engaging products that entice users to play captivating games and earn real rewards. JusDice is part of the applike group ecosystem, backed by strong financial support from Bertelsmann, and boasts a highly motivated workforce.

We are a team of experts in software development, online marketing, data-driven business intelligence, and app & game design. We are always seeking new creative solutions to complex problems, which has enabled us to release new products fast!

What you will do:

  • Developing robust, scalable, and efficient microservices in Go
  • Planning, developing, and maintaining backend architectures using AWS Cloud Technologies, Kubernetes, Go Microservices and ElasticSearch.
  • Ensuring quality through unit and integration testing
  • Collaborating within agile teams to analyze and implement product requirements
  • Optimizing existing systems for performance and scalability

Who you are:

  • Extensive experience in software development with Golang (at least 2–3 years)
  • Strong knowledge of AWS cloud services: mainly ECS, DynamoDB, CloudWatch
  • Working knowledge of Kubernetes
  • Proficiency with Terraform, Terragrunt
  • Proficiency in working with both relational and noSQL databases
  • Hands-on experience working with Microservices architecture
  • Solid understanding of software architecture and design patterns
  • Experience with CI/CD and DevOps practices
  • Strong sense of ownership, ability to work well in a team, and enthusiasm for innovative technologies
  • Be fluent in English
