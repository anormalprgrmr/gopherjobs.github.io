Description

Distribusion is the world’s leading ground transportation marketplace and gives travellers seamless access to ground transportation online, from search to ticket purchase. We have built a cutting-edge B2B technology platform that connects bus, rail, and ferry operators in 70+ countries with the biggest online retailers, including Google Maps and Booking.com.

We are shaping the future of travel and building the largest global network of transport providers and retailers. We are one of the fastest growing startups in travel, backed by two leading VCs (TQ Ventures & Lightrock), and now, following our recent $80m Series C funding, we are ready to push beyond.

Every day, millions of travelers book buses, trains, ferries and transfers through platforms they know, and many of those bookings run on Distribusion. We connect hundreds of carriers with the world’s leading travel retailers, making ground transport as easy to book as a flight.

We’re looking for ambitious graduates who want real responsibility from day one. As part of our Graduate Program, you’ll join one of our core teams as an Analyst or Engineer, work on live products used across the globe, and learn alongside people who are building the infrastructure of ground travel.

Workplace: Berlin & Remote or willing to relocate to Berlin.

Where you could make your mark Product: Analyst roles

  • Search Engine & Booking Platform: Build the core engine that returns fast, scalable search results and handles thousands of booking requests per second.
  • Supply & Integrations: Connect hundreds of external carrier APIs and help build robust, fault‑tolerant infrastructure that keeps bookings flowing.
  • E‑commerce: Shape the booking engines, payment flows and user journeys that let travelers search, book, pay for and manage trips. Your work directly moves conversion and retention for a global audience across many markets, currencies and languages.
  • Connectivity: Build enterprise‑grade integrations with external systems like Salesforce, SAP, CRMs and government authorities, with a strong focus on automation and innovation.

Tech: Engineer roles

  • Demand Engineering: Work on the heart of our platform: the search and booking engine that lets the world’s biggest travel retailers sell bus and rail tickets through a single API, and the schedule system behind hundreds of thousands of daily departures used by hundreds of operators. Build high‑throughput microservices, help modernize our core search engine, and shape the product, not just the code.
  • Content Engineering: Build the pipelines that keep global travel data accurate and up to date. Automate how we ingest stations, prices and schedule changes in real time, and apply AI to keep millions of data points reliable at scale.

Partnerships roles

  • Regional Partnerships: Work hands‑on with bus, rail, ferry and transfer operators and leading travel retailers in your region, guiding them from first integration to growing their bookings across our global network.
  • Corporate Partnerships: Bring ground transport into business travel by working with travel management companies, corporate travel agencies and booking tools, from technical onboarding to growing the partnership.
  • Performance & Growth: Dig into partner data, spot opportunities, and build the processes and automation that help us scale.
