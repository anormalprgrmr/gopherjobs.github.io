Key Engineer (Staff / Principal Level) – Backend, Infrastructure & IoT

Berlin | Full-Time | Start ASAP

We're looking for an exceptional engineer. A true A-player who wants to take ownership, build world-class technology, and play a key role in shaping the future of Heidi Systems.

About Heidi Systems

Heidi Systems is building the data infrastructure layer for buildings and energy.

We're transforming one of Europe's largest and most outdated industries by digitizing metering, consumption data, reporting, and billing. Our platform connects physical infrastructure with intelligent software, making buildings smarter, operations more efficient, and energy data accessible in real time.

We're a fast-growing, venture-backed startup with real customers, real traction, and ambitious plans to scale.

Now we're looking for a Key Engineer to become one of the technical pillars of our company.

This is not a traditional engineering position. We want someone with exceptional technical depth, strong architectural judgment, and an entrepreneurial mindset. Someone who doesn't just execute tasks but takes complete ownership of complex problems and builds solutions that last.

You'll have the autonomy to make important technical decisions, challenge existing approaches, and help define the engineering foundation of Heidi Systems.

What You'll Own

You'll take ownership of critical parts of our technology and infrastructure, working directly with the founding team.

  • System Architecture: Design and evolve scalable, reliable backend and infrastructure architectures that can support rapid growth.
  • Backend Engineering: Build and optimize high-performance services using Go and TypeScript.
  • Data Infrastructure: Own the ingestion, validation, processing, and transformation of large volumes of meter, gateway, and building data.
  • DevOps & Production: Take responsibility for our production infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, deployments, containers, and servers.
  • Database Engineering: Architect and optimize PostgreSQL environments, including performance, backups, replication, and reliability.
  • IoT & Connectivity: Solve complex challenges involving MQTT, wireless gateways, device communication, APIs, and real-world hardware.
  • Reliability & Monitoring: Establish robust monitoring, alerting, logging, and incident-response processes.
  • Engineering Excellence: Define technical standards, improve development processes, and build the foundation for a world-class engineering organization.
  • Technical Leadership: Work closely with the founders on major architectural decisions and help shape our future engineering team.

You're not here to maintain someone else's architecture. You're here to help build the architecture of a company that intends to become a market leader.

Who We're Looking For

We're looking for a top-tier engineer. Not simply someone with years of experience, but someone with an exceptional track record of building and owning complex systems.

You combine deep technical expertise with speed, pragmatism, and an uncompromising commitment to quality.

You don't need someone to tell you what to do every day. You identify problems, make decisions, take initiative, and deliver outstanding results.

  • Extensive experience with PostgreSQL or comparable relational databases
