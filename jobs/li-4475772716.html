Deine Aufgaben

  • Reinforcement of the developer team in the area of our video platform development / sports analytics
  • Design and implementation of innovative, complex, powerful and scalable backend solutions.
  • Support product development in all phases (architecture, technical design, implementation, testing and integration, maintenance and support).

Dein Profil

  • Degree in computer science or a comparable qualification.

  • Experience in the application of machine learning techniques
  • Knowledge of runtime optimisation, Cuda, OpenCV and/or PyTorch is a plus
  • Experience in desktop application development with C++, C#, or Java is considered an advantage.
  • Strong conceptual and analytical thinking
  • The ability to empathise with customers
  • Communication skills, team orientated thinking, problem solving skills are desirable
  • Very good written and spoken English skills
