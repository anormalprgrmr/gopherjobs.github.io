Description

About Us

Project Q is a European defence tech company with offices in Munich and Berlin. We connect existing sensors, effectors and command systems – regardless of manufacturer – so that armed forces, security agencies and critical infrastructure operators can act faster, from detection to response. Our integration and orchestration software HYDRIS is built on an open-source core and designed for environments without stable connectivity.

Your role

You'll build the bridge between Hydris and the real-world systems it has to work with. Hydris is our orchestration product, the coordination engine for sensors and mission systems. Those systems include radars, AIS receivers, LoRa mesh radios, satellite feeds, C2 platforms, and whatever our customers bring next.

The job is to turn heterogeneous hardware and protocols into clean, reusable integrations, so Hydris becomes more capable with every deployment. The work is hands-on and fast-paced. The problems lie in protocols, data modelling and system interfaces. They play out in environments that are bandwidth-constrained, intermittently connected, and operationally unforgiving.

What you'll do

From field to product

  • Work closely with our Forward Deployed Engineers and turn their field insights into improvements to our integrations.
  • Decide together with the Product team which integrations come next and how they feed into the product roadmap.
  • Occasionally step in for a Forward Deployed Engineer at a customer site.

Integration development

  • Design and build integrations that connect Hydris to new sensor types, data sources, effectors and command systems.
  • Work with real hardware and real protocols, often with incomplete documentation, and work out how to make them talk to Hydris reliably.
  • Take ownership of integrations that must run reliably on edge hardware, in disconnected networks, and alongside systems we don't control.

Integration Library and architecture

  • Take pragmatic solutions built under time pressure in the field and rework them into maintainable, reusable components of the Integration Library.
  • Help set the patterns for how built-in integrations (ADS-B, AIS, ASTERIX, Meshtastic, SpaceTrack, TAK, federation) are structured, tested, versioned and maintained.
  • Continue refining the integration framework with the Hydris Core team, including the schema and APIs that every integration depends on.
  • Make it straightforward for external developers and system vendors to build their own plugins/integrations to Hydris.

Technical leadership

  • Contribute to technical standards for integrations: testing against hardware and protocols we don't own, handling versioning, and keeping backward compatibility.
  • Review code with a focus on long-term maintainability and operational correctness, not just feature completion.
  • Contribute to build-versus-integrate decisions and help the team clearly understand the trade-offs.

  • Work comfortably in several languages and pick up new ones quickly. Hydris is built in Go and TypeScript with a touch of Rust, but integrations often comes with whatever the other side speaks (C/C++, C#/.NET, Java, Kotlin, Python, JS, and more).   • Are happy to spend much of your week in our Berlin-Tegel Engineering hub and to travel occasionally, on average up to 50% of your time.

  • Familiarity with defence or ISR data standards like CoT/TAK, ASTERIX, SAPIENT, MIL-STD-2525.
