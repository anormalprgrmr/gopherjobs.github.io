Description

This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a .NET Engineer Middle based in Germany. Join a remote engineering team building scalable, high-traffic technology for a fast-moving fintech environment.

You will design, develop, and maintain reliable backend solutions using C# and modern .NET technologies.

The role combines hands-on software development with distributed systems, data-intensive workloads, APIs, and cloud-native infrastructure.

Your work will directly contribute to products where technology, data, performance, and reliability are critical to the user experience.

You will collaborate with engineers and cross-functional stakeholders to turn complex business requirements into clean and maintainable solutions.

There is also significant scope to improve observability, engineering practices, and development efficiency through modern tools and AI-assisted workflows.

This opportunity is well suited to a mid-level engineer who enjoys solving challenging technical problems and working with modern backend technologies.

Accountabilities

  • Design, develop, and maintain scalable solutions for high-traffic systems using C# and modern .NET.
  • Analyze business and technical requirements and translate them into clean, maintainable components aligned with established architectural principles.
  • Collaborate with engineers and cross-functional stakeholders to design, implement, and deliver new product features.
  • Write clean, testable, maintainable, and well-documented code supported by appropriate unit, integration, and load testing.
  • Work with asynchronous programming, multithreading, distributed systems, and event-driven architectures to build reliable services.
  • Investigate production incidents and improve system observability through effective use of logs, metrics, traces, and business data.
  • Contribute to performance optimization, scalability improvements, and overall system reliability.
  • Work with APIs, relational databases, caching systems, and messaging technologies as part of the broader technology ecosystem.
  • Research and evaluate new technologies, tools, AI capabilities, and engineering practices that can improve product quality and development efficiency.
  • Contribute to continuous integration and delivery processes and support reliable software deployment practices.
  • Participate actively in an Agile development environment, collaborating with teammates to continuously improve products and engineering workflows.

Requirements

  • 3+ years of commercial software development experience using C# and modern .NET.
  • Strong understanding of object-oriented programming, design patterns, data structures, and algorithms.
  • Hands-on experience with multithreading and asynchronous programming.
  • Proven experience building and operating software in production environments.
  • Familiarity with distributed systems and event-driven architectures, including message brokers such as RabbitMQ or Apache Kafka.
  • Understanding of scalability and reliability principles, with some practical exposure to performance tuning.
  • Solid experience with relational databases, preferably PostgreSQL; familiarity with NoSQL databases is an advantage.
  • Experience developing and consuming APIs using technologies such as REST, GraphQL, or gRPC.
  • Hands-on experience with Entity Framework.
  • Practical experience with Docker and a basic understanding of Kubernetes concepts.
  • Experience writing and maintaining unit and integration tests.
  • Experience with continuous integration and delivery tools, preferably GitLab and ArgoCD.
  • Comfortable working in a fast-paced Agile environment and collaborating effectively with distributed teams.
  • Understanding of modern AI capabilities and limitations, with hands-on experience using AI-powered coding assistants or agents.
  • English proficiency at B1 level or higher, sufficient for technical discussions and documentation.
  • Experience with ClickHouse or other analytical databases for large-scale data processing is a plus.
  • Experience with AWS or another major cloud platform is a plus.
  • Familiarity with Infrastructure as Code tools such as Terraform is advantageous.
  • Experience in fintech, trading, or other high-load environments is a strong plus.
