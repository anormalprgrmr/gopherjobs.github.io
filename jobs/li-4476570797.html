Description

We’re flexible! We’re happy to receive applications in English or German.

Your tasks:

We are looking for an experienced Cloud Architect (f/m/d) to join our international team to design, develop, and maintain our cloud-based software solution. As a highly regulated company, we have to provide data to the regulatory authorities in defined time windows. We developed a cloud-native software for our internal business unit in Google Cloud Platform (GCP). You will support the project which is characterized by high complexity and variability with regard to the programming languages and frameworks used. In addition, you will assist in the further development and training of junior developers.

Our Tech-Stack:

  • Terraform
  • Github, Github Actions
  • PySpark-Datapipeline (Dataproc, Cloud Composer)
  • Spring Boot Services (CloudRun)
  • React Frontend
  • Databases: BigQuery, CloudSQL (PostgreSQL)
  • Pub/Sub as messaging service
  • Jira, Confluence

We work in an agile team and coordinate in dailies, refinements and sprint reviews. You decide whether you work remotely from home (Germany) or in our office in Leipzig.

After all, we value the exchange of ideas, being there for each other and encouraging each other to become better. We call it: the exchange mindset.

Your skills:

  • You successfully completed an IT-related university degree or a similar apprenticeship
  • You bring along very good expertise in the following programming languages and frameworks: Java, Python, Terraform, HTML, JavaScript, SQL
  • You are well experienced in working with backend frameworks like Spring Boot
  • You already designed cloud native applications (GCP is preferred), using the "infrastructure-as-code" approach
  • You write clean, efficient, reusable, and well-documented code and help establishing best practices
  • You can communicate excellently in English, ideally also in German

Why join us:

  • Attractive salary package with many advantages such as childcare, meal allowance, job ticket, sports and leisure events
  • Flexible hybrid work concept and flexible working hours
  • Personal development through extensive training opportunities
  • A place in a dynamic and international team within EEX Group and Deutsche Börse Group
  • A long-term perspective in the constantly growing and evolving energy industry
  • Bespoke onboarding plan
