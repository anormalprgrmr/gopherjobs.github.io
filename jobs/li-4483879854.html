Description

Location: Munich – on-site / hybrid, up to 60% remote

Salary range: €65,000–80,000 per year

We build and operate large-scale web crawling and extraction systems that turn unstructured, dynamic websites into clean, structured and accurate data. You’ll own crawlers end-to-end: from discovery and robust fetching against anti-bot defenses to turning HTML/PDF into reliable, structured data.

What you’ll do

  • Build and scale crawlers for large, dynamic websites – and keep them running when those sites change and fight back.
  • Stay one step ahead of anti-bot measures: TLS/browser fingerprinting, proxies, session and rate-limit strategy.
  • Turn HTML and PDF into clean, accurate, structured data, including LLM-assisted extraction. Our rule: silently wrong results are worse than a crash.
  • Design and run pipelines for ingestion, change detection, deduplication, versioning and enrichment.
  • Ship and operate containerized apps in the cloud, with CI/CD.
  • Help shape our technical architecture and engineering culture.

Our stack

Python, Docker, GCP (Cloud Run Jobs) and Azure Blob, MongoDB. The user-facing app consists of Go services and a React frontend. You don’t need to know everything on day one, but you do need to get up to speed quickly.

What we’re looking for

  • 3+ years of professional software development experience (5+ for the senior track).
  • Strong Python skills: real production code, async.
  • Hands-on web scraping experience, including first-hand experience with anti-bot defenses and common failure modes.
  • A data-correctness mindset: you care that the extracted data is right, not just that the job ran.
  • Docker plus at least one major cloud (GCP, Azure or AWS), Git, and confidence on the command line.
  • Clear communication and strong collaboration in a small, fast-moving team.

Nice to have

  • LLM-assisted extraction / experience with LLMs, ML pipelines or data-labeling workflows.
  • Frontend (React) experience, or the motivation to learn it.
  • Terraform and modern DevOps tools.

Why join us?

  • A lively team culture in our Munich office – with the option to work remotely up to 60% of the time.
  • Flexible working hours.
  • 30 vacation days per year (including 4 “Company Rest Days” over Christmas).
  • 30 days of workation per year – within the EU and in selected other countries.
  • Lots of ownership and flat hierarchies.
  • EGYM Wellpass with unlimited access to fitness classes and gyms.
  • Access to Udemy for online learning.
  • A modern company pension scheme in partnership with Ginkgo bAV.

About Us
