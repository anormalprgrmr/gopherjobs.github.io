Your Role As Senior DevOps Engineer, you will design and operate the cloud infrastructure that powers our in-game AI. Your main focus is the inference layer behind our LLM-driven gameplay, which means GPU hosting, model serving, and the surrounding backend needed to run language models reliably at scale.

Responsibilities

  • Design, build, and operate the cloud infrastructure for LLM inference and AI workloads
  • Set up and manage GPU-based hosting and model serving environments
  • Build reliable, scalable backend services that connect the game to our AI systems
  • Monitor performance, latency, and cost, and continuously optimize the stack
  • Automate deployments, scaling, and recovery wherever possible
  • Work closely with engineering to ensure infrastructure meets gameplay and product requirements
  • Evaluate and select the right cloud providers and tools as the stack evolves
  • Maintain strong security, reliability, and operational standards across all services

Requirements

  • 3+ years of experience in DevOps, SRE, or backend infrastructure roles
  • Strong Python skills
  • Solid Linux and Bash expertise
  • Experience operating production cloud infrastructure
  • Bonus: experience with GPU infrastructure or LLM model serving
  • Ability to work onsite in Munich, Germany
  • Valid EU work authorization
